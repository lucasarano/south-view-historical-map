# Cards quote the team notes word for word

A card shows the team notes' own words, with the page they come from, instead of a summary in our words. Quoting keeps the map from drifting away from the source: nothing is reworded, so nothing can be reworded wrongly. The cost is longer cards that keep the book's wording, including hard words about the 1902 riot and the 1922 casket thefts.
