# Assume a person rests at South-View unless something says otherwise

When the team notes don't say where a person is buried, the card says "Rests at South-View: Yes", as long as nothing we have says otherwise. Jacob McKinley is the one exception: the notes place him in his family's plot at Oakland Cemetery. The content sheet's "How we know where they rest" column marks every assumed "Yes", so an editor can tell a stated burial from an assumed one and correct it when a record turns up. We chose this because these people are on the map as part of South-View's story. Leaving their burial blank would have hidden them, or made them look less connected, without any evidence that they aren't there.
