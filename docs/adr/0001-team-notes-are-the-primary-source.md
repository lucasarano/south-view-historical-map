# The team notes are the map's source of truth

The map shows exactly the people and events in the team notes (`sources/team-notes.md`), connected the way the notes group them, and nothing else. The South-View documents (tour scripts and flyers in `sources/`) are never shown on the map. They are used only to find how a person in the notes is linked to South-View when the notes don't say. An outside source is used only to settle a disagreement between the two.

We chose this even though the South-View documents are the partner's own material. The tour scripts are written to be performed: first person, with invented lines, and with errors we found (a copied script, a birth year off by thirty years, two men named Lewis Watts merged into one). The team notes trace back to a history book with page numbers. All of the earlier prototype content was removed from the map for the same reason.
