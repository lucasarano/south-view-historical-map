# People named in the sources

Every historical person the scripts and flyers name, with dates and descriptions **exactly as each document gives them**. Nothing here is checked yet. Where two documents disagree, both are shown.

"Tie to South-View" quotes or points to what the document itself says. "Map #" is the numbered grave on the 1906 brochure's cemetery map.

## Names of those massacred in 1906 (1906 brochure)

Milton Brown, Frank Fambro, James Fletcher, Zeb Long, Leola Maddox, Sam Magruder, Will Marion, Will Moreland, Clem Rhodes, Sam Robinson, Annie Shepard, Frank Smith, William Welch, "11 Unnamed", "Countless Unknown".

The brochure lists these names but doesn't say where they are buried. The 1906 page also names Fred Walton, one of Herndon's workers, "run down by the mob and killed".

## Named inside the scripts with a stated South-View tie

- **Albert Watts**, "one of the founders", brother of Dinah Watts Pace (Pace script).
- **Lewis Watts**, "our brother", "the first secretary of the cemetery corporation" (Pace script).
- **Irvin "Mac" McDuffie**: Lizzie was "buried beside Mac, here at South-View" (McDuffie script).
- **Ariel Bowen**, J.W.E. Bowen's wife: "buried here with me" (Bowen script).
- **Flora Zeto Malekebu**: "Along with Flora, we rest here in a lot purchased by Spelman Seminary" (Clara Howard script).
