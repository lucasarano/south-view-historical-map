# Sources from South-View

The 20 documents South-View gave the team, turned into Markdown so they're easy to read, search and compare. **The words are unchanged.** Each file keeps the original text; only the layout moved (Word tables became paragraphs, PDF line breaks were joined). A header at the top of each file says where it came from.

Extracted on 2026-10-20 from the downloads of that morning. The original .docx/.doc/.pdf files are not in the repo.

**Private.** These are South-View's scripts and flyers. `sources/` is in `.vercelignore` so a deploy never publishes it.

## What's unexpected

### What kind of source these are
- **The scripts are tour performances, not biographies.** Each is a first-person monologue an actor performs at the grave, with stage directions, props and invented lines ("*winks*", "wait for crowd response"). J.W.E. Bowen's script talks about today ("first black president", "nine Black CEOs"). They're South-View's own material and good leads, but they're written to be performed, not cited.
- **The 1906 brochure is the only document with citations.** It cites page numbers in *Negrophobia* and *The Herndons*. Nothing else names a source.

### Missing or mixed-up files
- **The "Dr. Moses Amos" file is a copy of Ruby Doris Smith Robinson's script**, photo included. Only its first stage line was changed (Gate City Drug Store). There is no Moses Amos script.
- **Some files look like drafts.** Bennie Bell McLendon's file is "(AutoRecovered)1". Irene Thompson Dobbs's has highlighted passages (kept as `<mark>`), and we don't know what the highlighting means. Some file names have typos ("missionery", "First Negri dryggust ub Georgia" for "First Negro druggist in Georgia", "Takeway").
- **Several flyer people have no script:** Moses Amos, Jennie Williams, Edward R. Carter, Grace Towns Hamilton, and everyone on the Georgia Tech and 1906 lists except Dobbs, Howard, Turner and Bowen.

### Images
- **Inside the Word files: 2 photos.**
  - Clara A. Howard: the file's own label points to Wikimedia Commons ("ClaraAHoward1919").
  - Ruby Doris Smith Robinson: labeled "Image result for ruby doris smith robinson", so it was taken from a web search. The owner is unknown. The Moses Amos file has the same photo.
- **On the flyers: about 30 historical portraits with no photo credits.** Some are probably press photos (Hank Aaron in a Braves uniform, John Lewis at the Capitol).
- **Living people:** headshots and names of the actors (Deborah Strahorn, Vincent Cheeks, John Doyle, Shelby Woolridge, Justin Hall, LaDoris Bias-Davis, Ursula Renee Spratling).
- **Children:** names of Girl Scouts (Sage Daguiar, Aislinn Gilchrist, Amira Hadley, Madison Alsobrook, Mya Nelson, Nadia Baker).
- **None of these images should go on the map** without the owner's permission. The actors and children shouldn't go on it at all.

### Where the documents disagree, with each other or with our notes
- **How many founders.** South-View's flyers say "six" (the 1906 brochure and the Georgia Tech takeaway). Our live map says "nine".
- **Spelling.** The documents spell **McLendon**. Our live map says "McClendon".
- **Three conflicts with our team notes, now checked against outside sources** ([team-notes-check.md](team-notes-check.md)):
  - Howard's birth year: 1849 is right; the brochure's 1819 is a typo.
  - Robson's gift ($150 or $200): unresolved, so leave the amount out.
  - Lewis Watts: two different men. Albert's son was the secretary until 1936, and Dinah's brother was a Pullman porter.
- **George Union Wilder.** Our team notes' text for Wilder came from the 1906 brochure. The notes cite "p.19"; the brochure says p.198 of *Negrophobia*. The garbled words in our notes ("mu le-loading", "e was") come from the PDF's broken text layer.
- **Frank Smith.** He is on the brochure's list of the massacred. Lizzie McDuffie's script mentions "Sunny Boy Smith, a Western Union delivery man" murdered that night, and our team notes call Frank Smith a "telegraph office messenger". They may be the same man; no document says so.
- **Irene Dobbs.** Irene *Thompson* Dobbs (the wife, d. 1972) is a different person from "Irene Dobbs-Jackson" on the 2024 flyer, the name in our team notes (first library card, 1961).
- **Inside Ruby Doris Smith Robinson's script:**
  - She calls herself both "the new Executive Secretary" and "first female Executive Director".
  - It says SNCC was founded in 1961 at Shaw University (it was 1960).
  - It puts "water hoses" in the Montgomery Bus Boycott.
  - It says the "Freedom Fighters" gave her a scholarship.
- **Typos that change meaning:** "over $1000 veterans buried at South-View" (Georgia Tech takeaway); "Walt Fraiser" in Howard's script.

### Sensitive passages
- Howard's script: "It is rumored that I buried a lot of white victims that weren't counted... some right here at South-View." It's a rumor, stated as one.
- The 1906 brochure: Howard finding that a "black riot victim" was a white man in blackface (p.175-176).
- Irene Thompson Dobbs's script quotes Rev. Proctor's sermon blaming "black degenerates and idlers".
- The names of the 1906 victims.

## How it was made

- Word files: pandoc. The old .doc went through macOS `textutil` first.
- PDF script: poppler's `pdftotext`. Each script was then checked word for word against the original; the only differences are formatting.
- Flyers: typed from the page images, because their PDF text layer drops letters ("obbs" for "Dobbs").
