# South-View Historical Map

A map of the people and events connected to Historic South-View Cemetery in Atlanta, built for South-View by an LMC 3403 team. These are the words we use for its content and where that content comes from.

## Sources

**Team notes**:
The research the team collected from the South-View history book and other reading. This is where everything on the map comes from first.
_Avoid_: the info, the doc, research dump

**South-View documents**:
The tour scripts and flyers South-View gave the team. They only fill a gap in the team notes or confirm something in them; they never add new people to the map on their own.
_Avoid_: the downloads, the files

**Tour script**:
A first-person monologue an actor performs at a grave during a South-View walking tour. It is written to be performed, not cited.
_Avoid_: biography, bio

**Outside source**:
A source that is neither the team notes nor a South-View document (a newspaper archive, an encyclopedia, a court record). It is used to settle a disagreement between the other two.
_Avoid_: third source

## The map

**Entry**:
One person or one event on the map, with its own card.
_Avoid_: node, item, story

**Event**:
One of the moments the team notes group people under: the founding of South-View (1886), the Panic of 1893, the Pittsburgh riot (1902), the 1906 Atlanta Massacre, the casket scandal (1922) and the Civil Rights Movement (1954–1968).
_Avoid_: era, chapter, theme

**Connection**:
A line between two entries that the team notes state, such as a person and the event they're listed under, or an uncle and his nephew.
_Avoid_: link, edge, relationship

**Content sheet**:
The Google Sheet that holds every entry and connection. The map shows whatever is in it.
_Avoid_: database, the doc, the spreadsheet

**Editor**:
Anyone with edit access to the content sheet.
_Avoid_: admin, approver, maintainer

## People

**Founder**:
One of the six formerly enslaved men named in the team notes as founding South-View in 1886: Jacob McKinley, Charles H. Morgan, George W. Graham, John Render, Robert Grant and Albert Watts.
_Avoid_: co-founder, organizer, original stockholder (an original stockholder is not necessarily a founder)
